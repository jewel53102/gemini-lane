Lead Workflow
How an inquiry becomes a booked consult, a dated follow-up, a cold lead, or a new client — recorded the same way every time, so referral source and outcome survive all the way to closed revenue.
Pipeline at a Glance Precedes Client Workflow Stage 1

Nurture loops back into Qualify & Respond on the date the lead gave you. Cold is an endpoint — no further outreach unless the lead re-initiates contact. Converted exits this workflow and enters Client Workflow Stage 1 ↗ at its exit criteria: signed service agreement on file.
1Capture the Inquiry
Every inquiry — phone, email, web form, or a referral conversation — gets recorded the same way before anything else happens. This is the step that makes revenue attribution possible later; skip it here and it can't be reconstructed at consult.
Respond fast

First response within minutes of contact, regardless of channel. This is already the practice's standard today — the workflow exists to protect it, not to slow it down.

Log it immediately, before responding if it's quick

Name and contact info · referral source (the channel behind 90% of closed business — never skip this) · which service they're asking about (tutoring subject, advocacy, college counseling) · any timeline or urgency they volunteered · verbatim notes on anything specific they said. Recorded on the leads spreadsheet today; moves into Monday.com once it's built in Sprint 04.

Set status: New

Stays New until the first response goes out, then moves to Contacted.

Lead Status Reference
New

Inquiry received, not yet responded to.

Contacted

First response sent; waiting on the lead or mid-cadence.

Consult Scheduled

Time booked on the calendar.

Nurture

Real interest, wrong time or still undecided. Carries a dated follow-up, not an open-ended one.

Cold

Not a fit right now. No further outreach unless the lead re-initiates.

Converted

Signed the service agreement — now a client in Client Workflow Stage 1.

Nurture Cadence & Re-approach
Quiet-lead cadence

Day 2, Day 7, Day 21 — three touches before a non-responsive lead moves to Cold. Run manually today; becomes automated as part of the referral engine build in Sprint 08.

Seasonal re-approach

For Nurture or Cold leads sitting more than a term (roughly 3 months) without contact, one fresh touchpoint before they're treated as fully lapsed.

Manual today, automated later

This workflow describes what to do by hand right now, with the leads spreadsheet, Calendly, and PandaDoc. The quiet-lead cadence and revenue-by-referral-source reporting are planned builds — see Sprint 08, "The referral engine" — but the decision logic on this page doesn't wait on that build to be useful.
