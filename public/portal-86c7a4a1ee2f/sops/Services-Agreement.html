THIS SERVICES AGREEMENT (the "Agreement") is made on ________________________ (the "Effective Date") by and between ________________________________ (the "Client"), and OPTIMIND EDUCATIONAL STRATEGIES LLC, a Maryland limited liability company (the "Provider"). Recitals

WHEREAS, Client wishes to engage Provider for certain services (collectively, the "Services" as defined herein), and Provider agrees to provide the Services to Client in accordance with the terms and conditions of this Agreement.

Agreements

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises contained herein, and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged, and intending to be legally bound, the Parties agree as follows:

  1. Services. Client hereby retains Provider to non-exclusively perform those services indicated and described in Exhibit A herein (collectively, the "Services"). The Parties acknowledge and agree that during the Term of this Agreement, as hereinafter defined, the Services may be modified and/or expanded from time to time upon a written agreement executed by authorized representatives of the Parties expressly referencing this Agreement. Client acknowledges and agrees that its right to receive the Services hereunder is non-exclusive. Provider may provide the Services or such other similar services to any other third-party in its sole discretion and without liability to Client.
  2. Fees and Billing.
    1. Fees for Services Rendered. As full consideration for the provision of the Services, Client shall pay Provider fees at those rates described in Exhibit B herein (the "Fees"). Provider shall have the right to modify the Fees at any time during the Term, hereinafter defined, in Provider's sole discretion by providing Client with fifteen (15) day written notice of such Fee modification. The Client agrees to pay 2.99% credit card processing fee.
    2. Cancellation, Late, and No-Show Policy. In the event that Client cancels a Services session with Provider (a "Session") within 24 hours of the session, Client will be liable for payment of the full amount for such Session. Further, if a Client is more than twenty (20) minutes late to its scheduled Session, Provider has the right to consider the Session canceled, and Client shall be liable for payment of the full amount of such Session.
      Added — pending review If Client fails to join a scheduled Session, Provider will attempt contact by text message at five (5) minutes past the scheduled start time, and by phone call — to Client and, if Client is a minor, to Client's parent/guardian — at ten (10) minutes past the scheduled start time. If Client has not joined the Session within twenty (20) minutes of the scheduled start time, the Session will be ended and Client will be billed for the full Session fee as set forth above. Client and, if applicable, Client's parent/guardian will be notified by email, including a formal notice regarding time management. The same no-show billing applies if Client joins the Session but does not do so in compliance with the session-environment requirements described in Exhibit A — in that case the Session will be terminated and billed as a no-show. Provider tracks early cancellations, late cancellations, and reschedules as separate, distinct outcomes in Client's record.
    3. Electronic Communication. Emails and Text Messages that are, in Provider's sole discretion, brief in nature are considered standard best practice and will not result in Client being liable for payment to Provider. Emails and text messages that are, in Provider's sole discretion, more than a brief exchange will result in Client being responsible for payment of such email or text message at Provider's applicable rate. Phone calls with outside providers and Client, or his or her family, will be billed in half-hour increments.
    4. Payment of Fees. Provider shall prepare and submit an invoice to Client covering the total amount owed for Fees for Services provided as agreed upon in this Agreement on a monthly basis. Client agrees that, prior to receiving services outlined in the Services Agreement, Client will voluntarily file with OptiMind Educational Strategies valid credit card information for a credit card in their name. The Client hereby authorizes OptiMind Educational Strategies to charge the credit card provided and kept on file the balance of any outstanding invoices. Invoices will be sent out at the end of each month, and the Client's credit card will be charged for the full invoice amount on the date the invoice is issued. The Client agrees to maintain valid and up-to-date credit card information on file with OptiMind Educational Strategies. The Client acknowledges and agrees that this authorization will remain in effect until the Service Agreement is terminated or until the Client provides written notice to OptiMind Educational Strategies to cancel this authorization, allowing for a reasonable time to process such cancellation. The Client agrees to pay all charges incurred in accordance with the terms and conditions of this Service Agreement.
    5. Late Fee and Payment Failure. In the event that the credit card on file becomes inactive, invalid, or otherwise unable to process the payment, the Client will be notified and given 14 days to provide updated payment information. If the payment is not successfully processed within this period, OptiMind Educational Strategies reserves the right to charge a late fee (the "Late Fee") equal to twenty-five and 00/100 Dollars ($25.00) on the fifteenth (15th) day from the date of the invoice. In addition to the Late Fee, overdue payments shall bear interest from the fifteenth (15th) day from the date of the invoice at a rate of 1.5% per month, compounded monthly. Provider reserves the right to reject any additional request for Services until any outstanding balances have been satisfied by Client.
  3. Term.
    1. Term. This Agreement shall continue until the completion of the agreed upon Services under this Agreement (the "Term").
    2. Termination by Either Party. Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time for any reason, with or without cause, by notifying the other Party within five (5) days prior to the next scheduled Session.
    3. Obligations upon Termination. Termination of this Agreement for any reason shall not discharge either Party's liability for obligations incurred hereunder and amounts unpaid at the time of such termination. Client shall pay Provider for all Services rendered prior to the effective date of termination. Upon termination, each Party shall return the other Party's confidential information or personal property that is in its possession at the time of termination.
  4. Authorization to Disclose and Access Information. In order to allow Provider to furnish the Services in the most efficient and effective manner, contemporaneous with the execution of this Agreement, Client agrees to enter into that Authorization to Disclose and Access Information form attached hereto as Exhibit C.
  5. Relationship of the Parties. The relationship of the Parties hereto is that of an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement, and no course of dealing between the Parties, shall be construed to create or imply an employment or agency relationship or a partnership or joint venture relationship between the Parties or between one Party and the other Party's employees or agents. Provider is an independent contractor, and neither Party has the authority to bind or contract any obligation in the name of or on account of the other Party or to incur any liability or make any statements, representations, warranties or commitments on behalf of the other Party, or otherwise act on behalf of the other. If applicable, each Party shall be solely responsible for payment of the salaries of its employees and personnel (including withholding of income taxes and social security), workers' compensation, and all other employment benefits.

Exhibit A

Description of Services

Executive Function Coaching

The Specialist provides individualized coaching to strengthen executive functioning skills through targeted instruction, strategies, exercises, and activities tailored to the client's needs. Services may include support with organization, planning, time management, prioritization, study skills, emotional regulation, accountability, and the management of academic, professional, or daily-life demands. The Specialist may also meet with parents or guardians, recommend resources or tools, and collaborate with other professionals, as appropriate. Clients participating virtually are expected to attend sessions from a private, distraction-free environment using a stationary device and an appropriate work surface.

Special Education Advocacy

The Specialist provides consultation and advocacy to help families navigate the special education process. Services may include reviewing educational records, interpreting evaluations, providing recommendations regarding IEPs and Section 504 Plans, preparing for and attending school meetings, communicating with school personnel, and helping families make informed educational decisions. Advocacy services are consultative and do not guarantee any specific outcome.

College Counseling

The Specialist provides individualized guidance throughout the college planning and application process. Services may include college selection, application planning, essay coaching, executive functioning support, transition planning, disability accommodations guidance, and assistance identifying colleges that align with the student's goals, strengths, and support needs.
