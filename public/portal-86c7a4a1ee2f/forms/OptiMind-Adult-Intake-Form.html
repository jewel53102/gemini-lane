Full intake for a new adult client. Most of this can be filled in live during the intake session — some questions are conversational prompts meant to be talked through, not answered in one line.

How to use this file. Fill it in directly in the page. Everything saves automatically in this browser, so you can close it and pick up where you left off. Use the section links to jump around. When it's complete, use the buttons at the bottom to save a PDF, download the answers, or email them.

One thing to know about saving: progress is stored in this browser on this device only. Switching devices or clearing browser data will lose it — use Download first if you need a copy.
